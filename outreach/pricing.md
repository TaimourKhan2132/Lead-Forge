# Pricing

Use this only after someone replies. It never goes in a first email or a follow-up.

| Offer | Price | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter batch** | **$49**, paid upfront | 50 qualified leads in their niche and city, each with the observed gap, the evidence link, contact details and a suggested opener line. Delivered as a spreadsheet plus a printable report within 3 working days. |
| **Weekly batches** | **$99 / month** | 25 fresh qualified leads every week, no repeats, same format. Cancel any time. |
| **Custom pipeline or automation work** | Quoted per project | Scrapers, n8n workflows, data cleaning and enrichment pipelines built for their own process. Fixed quote after a short written brief. |

## Reply templates

**They said "yes" to the other 7.** Send the full pack (attach `packs/<domain>.html`, or paste the
Markdown), then one line:

> Here are all 10. If these are the kind of businesses you want more of, I can run 50 for $49 in the niche and city you choose. Want me to?

**They ask what it costs.**

> 50 qualified leads with openers is $49, paid upfront, delivered in 3 working days. If you want it ongoing, 25 a week is $99 a month. Anything custom (scrapers, n8n workflows, data pipelines) I quote per project.

**They ask how the leads are found.**

> Google Maps searches for your niche and city, then one visit to each business's own website to check for the gaps you can fix. Every gap in the report links to the page it was seen on. No bought lists.

**They ask for a call.**

> I work over email and Zoom. Happy to do a 15-minute Zoom, or I can answer anything here.

## Rules

- Quote the prices above as they are. No discounts in the first reply.
- Never promise results (meetings booked, revenue). The product is researched leads with evidence.
- Agree the payment method in the conversation. No payment details are kept in this repository.
